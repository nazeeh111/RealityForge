These recordings and parameters.json are a small transformed evaluation subset of **IRIS** by Rasul Khanbayov, Mohamed Rayan Barhdadi, Erchin Serpedin and Hasan Kurban.

Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/rasulkhanbayov/IRIS

Dataset revision: `c253822f55431ca80ef2084de4bc5e79d1a488f1`.

Changes: selected portions of take 10, downscaled from 3840x2160 to 960x540, frame rate reduced from 60 to 30 fps without changing playback speed, audio removed, H.264 encoding. Exact time windows, URLs, and SHA256 checksums are in `../provenance.json`. No IRIS recording is used for neural-network training. The short selected portions and small number of recordings do not constitute the complete official benchmark protocol.

Reference: Khanbayov et al., *IRIS: A Real-World Benchmark for Inverse Recovery and Identification of Physical Dynamic Systems from Monocular Video*, ECCV 2026 / arXiv:2603.16432. https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16432 .
